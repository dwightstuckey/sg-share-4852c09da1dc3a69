This is the heart of the program. StudioGuard is built in layers, and Professional Liability is the base.

Professional Liability covers errors, negligence and failure to perform. Media Liability sits on top with its own sub-limit for claims tied to published content. General Liability handles the physical world: shoots, events, on location. Business Property covers cameras, lighting, computers and studio gear. Platform Income Interruption keeps income flowing after a covered interruption. Equipment Warranty repairs or replaces an eligible device, up to $1,500 per device. And the Cyber Suite handles breach response, cyber extortion, payment fraud and identity recovery.

Here's how it plays out. A videographer starts with editing at home: Professional and Media. Then they start shooting on location, so GL. They rent gear, so property. They store client footage, so cyber. Each step in the business adds a layer.

Platform Income Interruption depends on the covered triggers. It isn't a promise that every suspension or demonetization qualifies. Available layers depend on class, eligibility and policy terms.

Insurance is one part of managing risk. Prevention is another. That's why we embedded TRiMRisk right in the StudioGuard application. We call it Prevention as a Service.

Why embed it? Four reasons. One: prevention is an essential element of a risk management plan. It's one of the four parts, and most small businesses skip it. Two: lower loss ratios. Three: higher retention, because a client using prevention every month has another reason to stay with you. Four: relationships. Certified service partners install the technology and a human service team stands behind it, so service providers and technology advisors join you on the account.

Look at the picture at the bottom. The small business owner is at the center. Around them: the technology advisor who recommends the right technology, the certified service partner who installs it and supports it, and you, the insurance agent who protects the business. TRiMRisk is the prevention partner that connects the three of you. Each of you has a seat at the owner's table, and each of you helps prevent the next claim.

TRiMRisk covers five areas: physical security, cyber security, driver safety, worker safety and smart operations. The client picks the areas that fit how they run.

Keep the line clear with your client: TRiMRisk is a separate subscription. It is not insurance and it does not establish coverage.
